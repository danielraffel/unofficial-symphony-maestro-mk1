What Maestro Is

Maestro is an independent, unofficial project for using Apogee Symphony I/O Mk I hardware on current macOS: the Symphony I/O over USB, and Apogee's ThunderBridge and Symphony 64 PCIe cards (PCIe support has had little testing). It is not made, endorsed or supported by Apogee Electronics. Apogee and Apple trademarks are used only to identify compatible hardware and platforms.

What It Installs

Maestro installs system components that need an administrator password: an audio driver, a driver extension for ThunderBridge and PCIe cards, and a USB helper. Installing or updating them can briefly interrupt audio. Updates are never installed without your confirmation. Versions on the Beta update channel are still being tested and may have bugs.
