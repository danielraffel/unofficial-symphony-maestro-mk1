Privacy Policy

Effective date: February 2026

Data Collection

Maestro does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. The app communicates exclusively with locally connected Apogee Symphony I/O hardware over USB.

Network Access

Maestro does not make any network connections. All communication is local between the app and your audio hardware.

Analytics

Maestro does not include any analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting frameworks.

Local Storage

Maestro stores user preferences (presets, window state) locally on your Mac using standard macOS mechanisms (UserDefaults, ~/Library/Application Support). This data never leaves your computer.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer via the project repository.
