Unofficial Symphony Maestro

Community-developed control software for the Apogee Symphony I/O MK I

⚠️ Experimental software with very limited testing. Use entirely at your own risk.

This will install:

  • Maestro.app — control app (volume, routing, metering), including the Symphony ThunderBridge driver (activates only when a ThunderBridge is connected — up to 32 channels over Thunderbolt)
  • Audio Server Plugin — 16-channel virtual audio device (USB)
  • Volume Proxy — background service for USB hardware control

Requires Apple Silicon and macOS 26 (Tahoe) or later. Intel is not supported.

ONLY tested on a single Symphony I/O MK I over USB and Thunderbolt (via the Apogee ThunderBridge), with 8x8 Analog-Optical and 16x16 AD Optical I/O boards.

Recommended firmware: Symphony 5.4C. That is the version Maestro was tested against; other versions likely work. Official Apogee software for the MK I is available from apogeedigital.com/download-files.
